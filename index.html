Privacy Policy — ScrollBreak

App: ScrollBreak: Beat Scrolling • Package: com.scrollbreak.app • Developer: ProDev Infotech • Effective: October 23, 2026 • Version 1.1.0
On-Device Guarantee: ScrollBreak's core features operate 100% on-device. We never sell data and run no advertising or analytics SDKs. The sole exception is ScrollBreak Pro billing (Section 8): purchases are processed by Google Play and verified by RevenueCat. Play Data Safety: purchase history and device identifiers are collected only for Pro billing; nothing else leaves your device.

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

ScrollBreak is built to foster mindful digital wellbeing and intentional habits. We believe that personal focus and digital privacy should never be compromised. Apart from optional Pro billing (Section 8) and the optional public-holiday lookup described in Section 3, the app functions entirely locally on your device — no user accounts, no cloud database of your habits, no advertising trackers, no telemetry analytics.

2. Third-Party Data Sharing

  • No data brokers or ad networks: we do not sell, rent, or transmit your usage habits, blocked-app list, or device data to any marketing network or data broker.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs: there are no third-party telemetry tools, tracking frameworks, or advertising libraries embedded in ScrollBreak.
  • Billing processors only: if you buy ScrollBreak Pro, Google Play processes the payment and RevenueCat verifies the purchase (Section 8). No other third party receives anything.
  • No cloud sync: your usage records, challenge completions, and configuration stay inside your device's sandboxed storage.

3. Android System Permissions & Purpose

Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

Purpose: used exclusively to detect foreground window and application changes. When you open an app you selected for friction, ScrollBreak detects the event and presents a mindful pause challenge (coloring canvas or cognitive puzzle).

Your consent: before Android Accessibility Settings are ever opened, the app shows an in-app disclosure dialog explaining this use. Tapping "I understand — Continue" records your consent on-device and opens system Settings; tapping "Decline", going back, or dismissing records nothing and changes nothing.

Privacy guarantee: ScrollBreak does NOT read personal messages, capture keystrokes, inspect screen content, access passwords, track user input, or transmit any accessibility data off your device.

Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

Purpose: query Android's local UsageStatsManager to compute minutes spent inside selected apps and mindful usage-free durations.

Privacy guarantee: all durations and timestamps are computed strictly on-device and are never uploaded or synced.

Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Purpose: display the mindful challenge screen over a restricted application when launched.

Privacy guarantee: used only to render the challenge UI; it does not monitor or capture content underneath.

Installed App List (launcher-visible apps only)

Purpose: list your launchable applications so you can choose what to monitor. No broad package-visibility permission is requested.

Privacy guarantee: your installed app inventory is never stored externally, profiled, or shared.

Ignore Battery Optimizations

Purpose: prevent OEM battery managers from terminating background detection during active sessions. No data use.

Internet (INTERNET)

Purpose: (a) user-directed app updates and update checks, (b) an optional public-holiday lookup that sends only a country code and year to keep festival spotlight dates accurate — cached for 7 days, with silent fallback to built-in dates on any failure, and (c) Google Play billing and RevenueCat verification if you buy Pro.

Privacy guarantee: never used to transmit telemetry, analytics, or personal profiles. No personal data is sent to the holiday service.

Note: ScrollBreak 1.1.0 does not run a foreground service. Background detection uses the AccessibilityService only, with no persistent notification and no remote monitoring.

4. What Data Is Stored Locally

  • Monitored app identifiers: package names of apps you choose to monitor.
  • Block interval settings: your chosen timeout intervals (e.g. 5 min, 15 min).
  • Challenge progress: selected challenge types and difficulty levels.
  • Local timestamps: last solve and last usage, used to compute session intervals.
  • Accessibility disclosure consent: whether you accepted the current disclosure text, and when, so the app only opens system Accessibility Settings after explicit consent.
  • Pro status cache: whether Pro is currently active and when it was last verified — used only to enforce the 2-app free limit while offline.

5. User Control & Data Deletion

  • Revoke permissions anytime: Android Settings > Apps > ScrollBreak.
  • Clear everything: Android Settings > Apps > ScrollBreak > Storage > Clear Storage, or uninstall. All local DataStore records are permanently erased.
  • No server copy: there is no account to close and no server-side profile to delete. Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat are deleted through their respective account flows.

6. Children's Privacy

ScrollBreak collects no data from anyone, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). The target audience is 13+. The app has no chat, no user-generated content, and an Everyone content rating.

7. Policy Updates

If we update this Privacy Policy to reflect new device capabilities, new billing or advertising integrations, or regulatory requirements, the change is published here with a new effective date and version, and summarised in the Play release notes.

8. Subscriptions, Billing & Purchase Data

ScrollBreak is free to use for up to 2 monitored apps. ScrollBreak Pro (Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime, or Founders Lifetime) unlocks unlimited monitored apps. This section applies only if you purchase Pro.

  • Merchant of record: all payments are processed by Google Play. Your card, UPI, or other payment credentials are handled exclusively by Google under the Google Play Terms of Service — they are never visible to us and never touch our systems.
  • Purchase verification (RevenueCat): to unlock Pro across reinstalls and keep subscription status accurate, the app sends purchase data to RevenueCat, our subscription-management processor. RevenueCat processes a pseudonymous app user ID, device identifiers, and purchase history (products purchased, subscription status, renewal dates) under the RevenueCat Privacy Policy (revenuecat.com/privacy). RevenueCat never receives your accessibility data, usage stats, or blocked-app lists.
  • What we store locally: only a cached Pro active/inactive flag and the time it was last checked, inside on-device DataStore. Clearing storage or uninstalling erases it.
  • Launch price, cancellation & refunds: there is no free trial. For eligible new customers, Google Play may apply a discounted launch price to the first billing period; the amount shown at checkout is the amount charged, and the standard price applies afterwards. All plans auto-renew until cancelled via Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. Refunds are governed solely by Google Play's refund policy. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Deletion: to erase purchase records held by Google or RevenueCat, use their respective account/deletion flows — we hold no server copy and cannot delete records we never stored.
