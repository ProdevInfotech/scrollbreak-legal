2. Purpose & Conscious Habit Friction

ScrollBreak is an intentional self-discipline and digital wellbeing application designed to help users break automatic, impulsive phone checking habits:

  • Mindful Pause: The app introduces a conscious friction pause (e.g., coloring a template, solving a cognitive puzzle) before unlocking selected distracting applications.
  • Self-Directed Configuration: You choose which apps to monitor, what challenge difficulty to set, and how frequently to enforce pause intervals.

3. System Permissions & User Consent

To function effectively, ScrollBreak requires user-granted Android permissions (all on-device only):

  • Accessibility Service: Used strictly to identify when a user-selected target application enters the foreground to display challenge friction. The Accessibility Service does not inspect personal content, record keypresses, or alter system inputs.
  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used locally to calculate application duration and time-free statistics.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used to overlay mindful challenges on top of chosen applications.
  • Installed Apps (launcher-visible only): Used to list launchable apps so you can choose what to monitor. Never sent off-device.
  • Ignore Battery Optimization: Used to maintain continuous guard reliability.
  • Internet: Used only to open the Play Store page when you tap Update. No telemetry.

You may revoke any permission at any time through Android System Settings, which may limit the app's capability to enforce friction pauses.
