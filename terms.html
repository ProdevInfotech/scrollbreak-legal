3. System Permissions & User Consent

To function, ScrollBreak requires user-granted Android permissions (all on-device except billing and the optional holiday lookup):

  • Accessibility Service: used strictly to identify when a user-selected app enters the foreground so a challenge can be shown. It does not inspect personal content, record keypresses, or alter system input. Before system Accessibility Settings are opened, the app shows an in-app disclosure dialog with "I understand — Continue" (accept) and "Decline" options; declining or dismissing records nothing and opens nothing.
  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): used locally to calculate app duration and usage-free statistics.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): used to overlay challenges on top of chosen apps.
  • Installed Apps (launcher-visible only): used to list launchable apps so you can choose what to monitor. No broad package-visibility permission is used. Never sent off-device.
  • Ignore Battery Optimization: used to maintain continuous guard reliability.
  • Internet: used for Play Store update links, an optional public-holiday lookup (country code and year only), and Google Play / RevenueCat billing verification. No telemetry.

You may revoke any permission at any time through Android Settings, which may limit the app's ability to enforce friction pauses.
